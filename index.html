My proposal to grow reach without paying for ads. Out of 20 units, 10 go on sale in-store and 10 are held for followers who share our post publicly and to their story. AI video ads · Crazy Clearance

How one listing became a 30-second ad

  1. Product prep: real price, RRP and features from the listing. Nothing invented.
  2. Story: clip 1 is the problem, clip 2 is the product fixing it.
  3. Kling 4.0 setup: 1080p, 10 seconds, 9:16.
  4. Clip 1: our own character, cartoon face, real-looking world.
  5. Check it: anything off? Redo it before moving on.
  6. Clip 2: starts from the last frame, so the story flows.
  7. Check the product looks exactly like the real thing.
  8. Voice: our founder's cloned voice, warm and British.
  9. Edit in DaVinci Resolve: music, features, price with the RRP crossed out.
  10. Export in 4K and post.

Our golden rule: AI tells the story, but the facts stay real. Price and text always go in at the edit, because AI still can't spell.
