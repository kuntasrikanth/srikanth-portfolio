Crazy Clearance · Audit · 2026

Store and website audit

Our main channel had 132,000 followers and 85 million reel views, and almost none of it turned into sales. Nobody asked me to, but I audited the website and socials, proposed an AI agent to answer comments that had gone unanswered, and made an in-store announcement with Fish Audio asking customers not to open sealed boxes. It was turned down, and I kept going.

Result Turned down at first, but the AI-agent idea became the reply bot I later built.
